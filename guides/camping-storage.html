Organising camping gear for easier trips

You do not need expensive equipment to make pack-up faster. A repeatable storage system does most of the work.

Quick checklist

  • Separate gear by activity
  • Dry equipment before storage
  • Secure heavy tubs in transit

Group by purpose

Keep cooking equipment, sleeping gear, lighting and first-aid items in separate, labelled bags or tubs. Pack things you need first where you can reach them.

Pick practical containers

Choose dimensions that fit your vehicle and campsite setup. Clear containers help identify contents; soft bags adapt to awkward spaces. Weather-resistant containers can help protect vulnerable items.

Manage weight and moisture

Put heavier items low and secure them for travel. Dry equipment before longer-term storage to reduce mould, corrosion and unpleasant smells.

Use a simple list

Record what is in each container and what needs replacing after a trip. A short checklist is more dependable than relying on memory.
