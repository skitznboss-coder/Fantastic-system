Portable power for camping: what matters

Power banks and portable battery units can be handy off-grid, but the right size depends on what you intend to charge.

Quick checklist

  • List your device power needs
  • Compare Wh where possible
  • Follow charging safety guidance

List your essential devices

Write down your phone, lights and any other necessary devices. Check their actual energy requirements and the number of times you want to recharge them.

Compare capacity honestly

Power bank capacity is often shown in mAh while larger batteries use Wh. Efficiency losses mean usable charging energy is less than the battery’s nominal capacity. Compare products using like-for-like units.

Check ports and recharge options

Look for compatible USB outputs and a suitable way to recharge the unit. A portable battery for a fridge has different requirements from a pocket power bank for a phone.

Prioritise safety

Buy from reputable suppliers, avoid damage and excessive heat, and use only manufacturer-approved accessories. Never operate fuel-burning generators inside a tent, car or enclosed space.

Before buying

Check current fitment, instructions, price, local requirements, seller details and returns on the retailer website. This guide is general information, not professional safety or mechanical advice.
