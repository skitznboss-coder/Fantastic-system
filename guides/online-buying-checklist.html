Quick checklist

  • Check compatibility first
  • Compare total delivered cost
  • Read returns and warranty terms

Verify compatibility

Read the model numbers, measurements and any fitment notes. Cars and electronic accessories are especially easy to mismatch by year, model or connector type.

Check the full price

Compare delivery costs, taxes and return postage as well as the item price. Some promotions have conditions and expiry dates.

Look at seller and return details

Check who is selling and fulfilling the order, what happens if an item arrives damaged and whether warranty support is available in Australia.

Use reviews carefully

Reviews can be useful when they describe long-term use and clear limitations. Do not treat a rating alone as proof that a product is suitable for your situation.

Before buying

Check current fitment, instructions, price, local requirements, seller details and returns on the retailer website. This guide is general information, not professional safety or mechanical advice.
