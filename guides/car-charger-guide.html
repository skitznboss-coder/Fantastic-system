Picking the right in-car phone charger

A reliable charger is useful for navigation and emergencies, but compatibility is the key buying question.

Quick checklist

  • Check your outlet type
  • Match your charging standard
  • Keep cables out of the driver area

Know your power outlet

Some cars have USB ports, some have a 12-volt accessory socket and others offer wireless charging. Check which outlet you actually have and its supported output.

Match cable and phone

Your phone may need USB-C, Lightning or another connection. A cable with the correct connector is necessary, and fast-charging performance depends on both the phone and power adapter.

Be realistic about charging speed

Higher advertised watts do not guarantee your phone will use all that power. Certified, reputable equipment and proper heat management are more important than large numbers on the packaging.

Install tidily

Keep charging cables clear of gear controls, footwells and steering components. Do not operate your phone while driving.
