A practical pre-road-trip checklist

A little planning can make longer drives more comfortable, safer and less expensive.

Quick checklist

  • Inspect the vehicle before leaving
  • Plan breaks and fuel or charging
  • Secure all luggage

Check the vehicle

Before you go, review tyres, lights, windscreen, fluids and any warning indicators using the vehicle handbook. Book a qualified inspection if something seems wrong.

Pack for minor delays

Consider drinking water, a charged phone, an appropriate first-aid kit, warm clothing and a torch. In remote areas, communications and emergency supplies matter much more than gadgets.

Plan rest and fuel

Work out fuel or charging stops, check opening hours in regional areas and allow for breaks. Fatigue is a serious risk: do not try to push through it.

Keep your load secure

Heavy items belong low and should be properly restrained. Avoid overloading the car and check your vehicle’s load ratings.

Before buying
