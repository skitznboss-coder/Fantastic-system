Dash cams: what to compare before buying

The best dash cam for you is one that captures usable footage reliably and is installed safely.

Quick checklist

  • Usable footage beats headline resolution
  • Plan cable routing carefully
  • Check local recording rules

Compare useful recording details

Resolution alone is not enough. Consider night recording, field of view, date and time accuracy, and whether the camera can read a number plate in typical conditions. Independent real-world footage tests are helpful.

Consider storage

Check supported memory card specifications and whether loop recording and card-health warnings are included. Video recording can wear out ordinary cards, so confirm the maker’s guidance.

Plan power and installation

Check whether the unit uses a plug-in adapter or requires a professionally fitted hardwire kit. Route cables so they do not obstruct airbags, the driver or controls.

Respect privacy

Check your state or territory rules on recording sound and sharing footage. Avoid mounting anything where it obstructs your view.

Before buying

Check current fitment, instructions, price, local requirements, seller details and returns on the retailer website. This guide is general information, not professional safety or mechanical advice.
