About AutoSmart Deals

A practical Australian buying-guide website about useful gear for cars, camping and the open road.

Our approach

We focus on clear explanations of features, compatibility, safety considerations and shopping questions. Readers should make their own checks before placing an order, especially for products involving road safety or electrical equipment.

How the website is funded

Some of our links are affiliate links, including links to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. We may receive a commission when an eligible purchase is completed; there is no extra charge to you. This does not guarantee a positive review or recommendation.

Independence and transparency

Information is intended to help you evaluate products, not to suggest we have purchased or personally tested items unless that is specifically stated. Prices, availability, delivery and support are controlled by the retailer.
